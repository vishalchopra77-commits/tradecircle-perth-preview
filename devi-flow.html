Rate Buddies · how a match becomes an alert

Automatic recommendation requests

1 Facebook group post

Someone asks for a plumber in Joondalup.

2 Devi

Watches the group and sends a short alert.

3 Rate Buddies webhook

Receives the link, group, snippet and time.

4 City match, same trade excluded

Joondalup maps to Perth. Michael (Plumber) is featured. Other plumbers are not alerted. 12 Perth members will be asked to recommend him.

5 AI agent notifies you

Grok Bot (or email, SMS, push) sends a card to recommend Michael.

6 You tap Approve

The only step you take. A signed one-tap link.

7 Auto publish + alerts

Recommend feed updates. Perth members (except plumbers) are asked to recommend Michael.

8 Members reply, then done

They vouch on Facebook only if they genuinely know his work, then tap “I recommended Michael”.

What you do, and what happens on its own

  • Review first is the default. Auto-publish for high-confidence matches is optional and can be turned on later.
  • Every member in the city is alerted, except members of the same trade (conflict of interest). A same-trade member is featured so others know who to help.
  • If no member of that trade lives in the city, the request cannot be approved — there is no one to help.
  • The approve button is a signed link, so Grok Bot, email or SMS can all complete the same step.
  • Members open Facebook themselves and only recommend people they genuinely know. The app never posts the comment for them.
