Rate Buddies — royal blue logo palette
======================================

Font
  Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold (weight 800)
  The wordmark files already have the letters converted to shapes,
  so you do not need this font installed in Canva. Use it only if
  you want to type the name yourself.

Colours
  Navy (dark backgrounds, RATE on light versions)   #0A1328
  Navy mid                                          #15244A
  Sky (start of BUDDIES on dark versions)           #3AA0FC
  Mark left (lightest buddy)                        #34A3FD
  Mark mid                                          #2082ED
  Mark right (deepest buddy)                        #2145B4
  Accent / primary blue                             #1A5FD4
  Deep indigo (end of BUDDIES on light versions)    #1E38B0
  Violet (end of BUDDIES on dark versions)          #7B7AE8
  White (RATE on dark versions)                     #FFFFFF

Wordmark colours
  Dark backgrounds:  RATE #FFFFFF,  BUDDIES #3AA0FC → #7B7AE8
  Light backgrounds: RATE #0A1328,  BUDDIES #1A5FD4 → #1E38B0

The four-buddy mark uses a left-to-right gradient from #34A3FD to #2145B4.
