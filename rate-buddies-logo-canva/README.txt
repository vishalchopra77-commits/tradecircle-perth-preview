Rate Buddies — Canva logo pack (royal blue)
===========================================

These files are ready to upload to Canva. There is a dark version
(for dark or photo backgrounds) and a light version (for white or
pale backgrounds).

How to use in Canva
-------------------
1. In Canva, click Upload, then upload the files in the png folder
   (and the svg folder if you want to recolour pieces).
2. Drag a file onto your design.
3. Transparent PNGs (names that do not say “on-navy” or “on-white”)
   have a see-through background, so they sit cleanly on any colour
   or photo.
4. Use “dark” files when the background is dark — RATE is white.
   Use “light” files when the background is light — RATE is navy.
5. The SVG files are vector, so they stay sharp at any size. The
   letters are already converted to shapes (the A in RATE has its
   crossbar). The buddies, RATE and BUDDIES are separate groups, so
   you can move or recolour them in Canva.

What’s in the pack
------------------
png/ and svg/ contain matching files:

  mark                     The four cheering buddies, transparent.
                           Works on light or dark.

  wordmark-dark            “RATE BUDDIES” for dark backgrounds.
  wordmark-light           “RATE BUDDIES” for light backgrounds.

  logo-stacked-dark        Buddies above the words, transparent, dark version.
  logo-stacked-light       Same layout, light version.
  logo-stacked-on-navy     Stacked logo already on a navy rectangle.
  logo-stacked-on-white    Stacked logo already on a white rectangle.

  logo-horizontal-dark     Buddies on the left, words on the right, dark version.
  logo-horizontal-light    Same layout, light version.
  logo-horizontal-on-navy  Horizontal logo already on a navy rectangle.
  logo-horizontal-on-white Horizontal logo already on a white rectangle.

  app-icon-1024            Square app icon, 1024 × 1024 pixels.

  palette.txt / palette.png  The official colours and the font name.
  preview.png                A contact sheet of every logo file.

PNG sizes are high resolution (logos 3000–4000 pixels wide, the mark
3000 pixels wide, the icon 1024 × 1024) so they stay sharp in print
and in Canva.

Notes
-----
• Keep the four-buddy shape as it is — this is the approved logo.
• The font is Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold. You only need it if you
  want to type new text. The logo files do not depend on it.
• The square app icon has space above and below the buddies because
  the four figures are a wide shape.
